Supported conversion families

The LibreOffice service supports the listed families. PDF supports TXT extraction and editable DOCX reconstruction through Ghostscript docxwrite. PDF-to-DOCX uses Ghostscript docxwrite to reconstruct text and layout heuristically; complex layouts, scanned pages and tables need review. It is not a pixel-identical conversion.

Conversion fidelity and security: “All formats to all formats” is not technically universal. This tool exposes genuine conversions supported by its engine, reports failures per file, enforces a 50 MB upload limit by default, and does not claim perfect fidelity. Run the service in a trusted environment; do not expose it publicly without authentication, rate limiting, TLS and appropriate isolation.
