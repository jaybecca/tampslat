How to price a service without guessing

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Calculate the full delivery cost. Include labour time, preparation, revisions, software, payment fees, taxes where applicable, overhead allocation and the cost of acquiring customers.

Start with the real need

Define the scope carefully. State deliverables, assumptions, client responsibilities, turnaround times and the number of revision rounds. Unclear scope creates hidden unpaid work.

Build a practical approach

Choose a pricing method that fits the service: fixed fee for defined scope, hourly or day rate for uncertain work, or a retainer for ongoing capacity. Check that the price supports the required margin.

Put it into action

Review actual delivery time and profitability after each engagement. Adjust the scope, process or price when evidence shows the original assumptions were wrong.
