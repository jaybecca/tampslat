How to write an SOP people will actually follow

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Choose one process and define where it starts and ends. Identify the role responsible, the required inputs and the expected output.

Start with the real need

Write steps in the order they happen. Use action verbs, short sentences and screenshots or examples where they remove ambiguity. Explain decision points and what to do when something goes wrong.

Build a practical approach

Include quality checks, safety considerations, escalation routes and records to keep. Avoid embedding passwords or confidential information in the document.

Put it into action

Test the procedure with someone who did not write it. Watch where they hesitate, revise those sections and assign an owner and review date so the SOP stays current.

Make the next step manageable
