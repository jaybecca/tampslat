How to run a productive project kickoff meeting

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Send a short pre-read with the project purpose, known constraints, proposed milestones and questions that need decisions. Invite only people who contribute or need the information.

Start with the real need

Confirm the outcome and how success will be measured. Clarify what is in scope, what is excluded, who approves changes and which dependencies could affect delivery.

Build a practical approach

Agree roles, communication channels, meeting cadence and escalation paths. Record decisions and unresolved assumptions rather than allowing them to remain implicit.

Put it into action

End with named actions and due dates. Share the notes promptly and use the project plan as the single source of truth for progress.

Make the next step manageable
