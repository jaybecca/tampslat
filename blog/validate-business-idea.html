How to validate a business idea before investing

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Define the customer and the problem narrowly. Identify the situation in which the problem occurs, how often it happens and what it costs the customer in time, money or frustration.

Start with the real need

Speak to potential customers using open questions. Ask how they currently handle the problem and what they have already tried. Listen for real behaviour rather than treating compliments as purchase intent.

Build a practical approach

Test a small offer: a landing page, paid pilot, prototype, sample service or limited launch. Set a measurable signal in advance, such as qualified enquiries, deposits or repeat use.

Put it into action

Review what you learned and decide whether to proceed, adjust the audience, change the offer or stop. Keep a record of assumptions so the next decision is based on evidence.

Make the next step manageable
