How to design a practical employee onboarding plan

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Prepare access, equipment, workspace and a clear first-week schedule before the start date. Assign a manager and a colleague who can answer everyday questions.

Start with the real need

Break learning into stages. Cover the organisation, role expectations, key tools, customers, processes and required training without overwhelming the new starter.

Build a practical approach

Set achievable milestones for the first 30, 60 and 90 days. Explain how progress will be reviewed and what support is available.

Put it into action

Ask the employee what is clear and what remains confusing. Use feedback to improve the process and confirm that mandatory policies are handled appropriately for your location.

Make the next step manageable

Choose one action you can complete this week, record what you learn and refine the process using real evidence. TAMPslat provides editable templates and browser-based business tools to help organise this work.
