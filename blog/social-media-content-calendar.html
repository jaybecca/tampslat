How to plan a useful social media content calendar

TAMPslat Journal · Practical guide

Choose a primary audience and one or two business goals, such as qualified enquiries, product education or customer retention. Select channels based on where the audience actually spends time.

Start with the real need

Create a small set of content themes. Mix educational explanations, proof of work, customer questions, behind-the-scenes context and clear offers. Avoid making every post a sales pitch.

Build a practical approach

Plan formats and production effort realistically. Record the topic, channel, owner, draft date, review date, publishing date and call to action.

Put it into action

Review performance against the goal. Look beyond likes to saves, useful replies, website visits and qualified enquiries. Repeat themes that help the audience and retire formats that consume time without value.
