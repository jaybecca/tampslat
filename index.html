BETTER INPUTS · BETTER BUSINESS DOCUMENTS

Bring the right details. Build a stronger document.

Use this checklist before you generate or edit a template. More complete information creates clearer, more useful drafts and reduces avoidable back-and-forth.

01

Purpose and audience

State the document type, who will read it, the decision you want them to make, and the problem or opportunity it addresses.

02

Business and brand details

Add your business name, contact details, logo, preferred tone, service description, relevant credentials and brand colours.

03

Scope, deliverables and timing

Describe the work included, what is excluded, milestones, responsibilities, dependencies, delivery dates and acceptance criteria.

04

Pricing and commercial terms

Specify currency, line items, quantities, taxes where applicable, payment milestones, due dates, validity period and cancellation terms.

05

Evidence and constraints

Include verified facts, source material, required sections, word limits, confidentiality needs, accessibility requirements and any must-use wording.

06

Review before sharing

Check names, dates, totals, legal or policy statements, assumptions and promises. Replace placeholders and confirm the final version with the right people.

This brief is a planning aid; it does not automatically populate a document yet.
