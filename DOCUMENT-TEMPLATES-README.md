# TAMPslat document template library

Ten editable, standalone HTML templates are included in `templates/`. Open Document & Business Studio in `tools.html` and choose a template card, or open a template directly. Replace placeholder text by clicking into editable areas, then use **Print / Save as PDF** to create a PDF. These templates are client-side HTML examples and do not save content to cloud storage automatically.

1. Professional CV
2. Cover Letter
3. Business Proposal
4. Standard Operating Procedure
5. Business Invoice
6. Project Brief
7. Meeting Minutes
8. Quotation / Estimate
9. Business Report
10. Purchase Order
