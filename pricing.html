Before you choose

Can I use the tools without subscribing?

The Free plan includes the monthly allowances shown above. Server-backed AI, OCR, CSV analysis and saved work are metered by the account service. Browser-only transformations are metered only when the studio submits a usage event.

Are the listed benefits all active now?

Plan limits for server-backed actions are enforced by the Worker after the usage migration is applied. Some specialist conversion engines and local-only browser operations have separate availability boundaries; the support page explains these limits.

How are AI credits priced?

The initial proposed unit prices are ₦4 per credit, $0.50 per credit and £0.37 per credit. EUR and CAD are estimates based on fixed display multipliers. Validate provider costs, taxes, currency rules and margins before launch.

Can I change or cancel a plan?

Paid plans currently grant 30 days after verified Flutterwave payment and do not renew automatically. Contact support for billing questions and review the Refund Policy before purchasing.
