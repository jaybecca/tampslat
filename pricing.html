Before you choose

Can I use the tools without subscribing?

Every plan includes access to every studio and browser-based tool. Paid plans increase the monthly limits for account saves and server-side CSV quality checks. Local file processing, including OCR, is not metered; tool-specific file formats and size limits apply. AI requests use separate credits.

Are any studios locked to a paid plan?

No. Every plan can access all studios and supported browser-based tools. Paid plans increase account-save and server-side CSV quality-check allowances; AI credits are separate, and local file processing is not metered. Your current totals appear in Settings and the avatar menu.

How are AI credits priced?

The catalogue rates are ₦4, $0.50, £0.37, €0.35 and CA$0.68 per credit. The final amount is calculated on the server in the selected currency. Credit top-ups are added only after the payment provider confirms the transaction.

Can I change or cancel a plan?

Flutterwave plan purchases in supported African countries provide 30 days of access and require manual renewal; they do not automatically renew. Paddle subscriptions outside Africa renew monthly. Manage or cancel a Paddle subscription through the provider’s customer portal or contact Support. Refund eligibility is described in the Refund Policy.
