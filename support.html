Frequently asked questions

How is my usage measured?

Monthly usage is measured by the account service for AI requests, OCR pages, CSV analyses, saved work and other server-backed actions. Browser-only tools process files locally where indicated. Open Usage & Limits to see this month’s counters. Limits reset on the first day of each calendar month in UTC.

How do I start using a tool?

Open the tools workspace, choose a tool card, add or enter your content, select an available action, review the result, then download or export it where supported.

How does ZIP File Processor work?

Select supported files or a ZIP archive, choose a supported archive action, and download the output. Password-protected and unusual archive formats may need additional support.

What can I do with PDF Studio?

Use the actions shown in the interface for supported PDF tasks such as combining files. Always check page order and output before sharing or signing a document.

How do I use Document & Business Studio?

Choose a template such as a CV, cover letter, proposal, SOP, invoice or report; complete the fields; review all details; and export the draft. Verify financial, legal and employment information yourself.

What does OCR do?

Optical character recognition extracts machine-readable text from supported images or scanned documents. Results can be imperfect, especially with blurry photos, handwriting or unusual layouts.

How do I clean a spreadsheet?

Import a supported CSV or spreadsheet file, choose available cleaning or duplicate-removal actions, inspect the summary, and download the cleaned result. Keep an original copy of important data.

How does Batch Studio work?

Add multiple files, choose a supported batch operation, review the processing summary, and download the generated results. Batch actions depend on file type and the operations currently available in the browser.

What can AI do in TAMPslat?

When secure provider credentials and available AI credits are configured, AI can help draft and refine business documents, summarise content, interpret spreadsheet data and suggest workflows. Each request uses one monthly plan allowance and AI credits. Review all generated output before relying on it.

How do AI credits work?

AI credit top-ups use Flutterwave for supported African markets after merchant secrets are configured. Credits are added only after the server verifies the transaction. AI requests require both an available monthly request allowance and sufficient credits.

How do I change the theme?

Use the theme toggle in the workspace header or avatar menu, or open Settings. Your light/dark preference is saved in this browser.

How do I reset or change my password?

Use the account password controls where available. The password-reset email flow still requires transactional email configuration. If you cannot access your account, contact support and never send your password.

Are my files stored in the cloud?

Browser tools process selected files locally where supported. Saved work items use the account service, but a full cloud file vault and R2 file storage are not enabled. Keep your own backups of important files.

How do plans and billing work?

Plans include monthly limits for studio actions, AI requests, OCR pages, CSV analyses, exports and saved work. Paid plans activate for 30 days after verified Flutterwave payment. Automatic recurring renewals, refunds and chargeback automation are not enabled.

Is the admin analytics dashboard live?

Account and usage summaries are read from the connected database. Revenue, country and regional reporting remain limited and should not be treated as complete financial analytics.

What if a file fails to process?

Check the format, file size and whether the file is damaged or password-protected. Try a small sample first and contact support with the action attempted and any non-sensitive error message.

Which file formats are supported?

Supported formats vary by studio. Check the accepted-format note beside each upload control before selecting files. If a format is not listed, convert a copy first and retain your original.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Many tools process files in your browser. Features that call an AI provider or a configured backend may send the selected text or file data to that service. The interface should identify those actions before you submit. Do not upload confidential or regulated information unless you are authorised to do so.

Can I use TAMPslat on a phone or tablet?

The public pages and supported tools are designed to adapt to smaller screens. Complex editing tasks such as spreadsheets, slide layouts and 3D asset work are generally easier on a larger screen.

How do language and regional settings work?

TAMPslat can suggest a language from your browser preferences and a region from Cloudflare's country signal. You can change the language and display currency manually. Location detection is approximate and should never override your explicit choice.

Does changing currency change the actual price?

Displayed prices outside NGN are estimates, not live exchange rates. Nigeria checkout defaults to NGN; other supported African markets may use USD. The final amount and available payment methods are shown on Flutterwave’s hosted checkout.

What happens when AI is unavailable?

Core file handling and document editing should remain available where supported. AI-assisted drafting, summarising, translation and refinement require a configured provider, an authenticated session and sufficient credits.

Can AI overwrite my original document?

AI output should be treated as a proposed revision. Review the result and choose whether to apply it. Keep an original copy before accepting edits or exporting a revised file.

How do I report a security or privacy concern?

Email tampslat@tampconsulting.space with the page or tool involved, the approximate time and a description of the issue. Do not include passwords, API keys, payment-card details or confidential document contents.

How do I request account deletion or data correction?

Contact support from the email address associated with your account and describe the request. Account deletion and correction workflows must be verified against the live account and retention configuration before a request is considered complete.

Where can I find plan, cancellation and refund information?

Review the Pricing page and the Privacy, Terms, Refund, Cookie and AI Use policies linked in the site footer. The current pricing and checkout experience is a proposal until the billing provider and subscription webhooks are active.

How do I convert a Word or Office document?

Open Document Converter Studio, select a supported source file and output format, then start the conversion. The dedicated conversion service must be running and reachable. Conversion fidelity varies by source document; inspect the result before sharing it.

How do I create or edit an EPUB ebook?

Open Ebook Studio to create a book, add chapters and sections, enter publication metadata, style the reading view and compile an EPUB 3 package. You can also import a supported EPUB and export readable chapter content. Keep your editable project JSON as a working backup.

Need further support?

Contact us directly. Never send passwords, full payment-card details, API keys or confidential documents in a support message.

Payments in Africa

Customers in supported African markets are routed to Flutterwave. Nigeria defaults to NGN and opens Flutterwave’s secure hosted checkout. TAMPslat activates a paid plan only after the server verifies the transaction. Keep your Flutterwave receipt and transaction reference. Never send card details, PINs or one-time passwords to support.

Keep an original copy, review converted or AI-assisted output, and avoid uploading confidential or regulated information unless you are authorised.

Replace bracketed prompts and verify facts before sharing. Templates are working aids, not professional legal, tax, accounting or HR advice.
