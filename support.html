Frequently asked questions

How do I start using a studio?

Open the workspace or Studio Directory, choose the task you need, select a supported file or enter your content, then review the result before exporting or saving it.

Which studios are available?

TAMPslat includes Document, Business, Career, Design, AI, File, OCR & Smart Vault, Spreadsheet, PDF, Presentation, Ebook, 3D and Batch workflows. The available actions and supported formats are shown inside each studio.

What counts toward my monthly usage?

Only successful account saves and server-side CSV quality checks count toward monthly plan allowances. Each successful save or CSV check uses one unit. Allowances reset on the first day of each calendar month (UTC); unused allowances do not roll over, and failed server requests do not count. Local browser processing, including OCR, is not metered. AI requests use separate credits.

Where can I see my current usage?

Open your avatar menu and choose Profile & settings, or open the Monthly usage section in Settings. You can see account saves, server CSV checks, AI request counts and your available AI credit balance.

How do AI credits work?

New accounts receive 5 one-time welcome AI credits. AI drafting and refinement use the separate credit balance shown in your account. The server deducts credits only after a successful response; failed requests are refunded automatically. Additional credits can be purchased separately and are added after payment verification.

What happens when I reach a monthly limit?

The server blocks additional account saves or server-side CSV checks after the relevant plan allowance is reached. You can upgrade or wait for the next UTC monthly reset. Browser-based file operations, including OCR, remain available; AI requests use a separate credit balance.

Are my files uploaded when I use a studio?

Many PDF, ZIP, spreadsheet, design and OCR actions process files locally in your browser. Account saves and server-side CSV checks send the content needed for that action to TAMPslat. AI prompts and selected document text are sent securely from TAMPslat to OpenAI. Do not submit confidential information unless you are authorised to do so.

What does OCR do?

OCR & Smart Vault runs OCR locally in your browser and can recognise supported images and PDF pages. Source files and extracted text stay on your device unless you explicitly choose an account-backed or AI action. Local OCR does not use a monthly plan allowance. Results can be imperfect with blurry photos, handwriting or unusual layouts, so review the text carefully.

How do I compare document versions?

Open Document Studio, paste or load two text versions, and select Compare versions. Added and removed wording is highlighted with change counts. The comparison is text-based rather than a pixel-level layout comparison; inspect the original files before approving important changes.

How do I use Career and Business Studio?

Career Studio groups CVs, cover letters, statements of purpose and portfolios. Business Studio focuses on proposals, quotations, invoices, business documents and finance workbooks. Choose a template, complete the fields and review every detail before exporting.

Which payment methods are supported?

Payment providers and available methods depend on your country and currency. Eligible Nigerian customers are directed to Flutterwave, which may offer cards, bank transfer and USSD where available; other supported regions use Paddle. The hosted checkout confirms the available methods. TAMPslat does not collect your full card or bank details.

How do plan renewals and cancellations work?

Nigerian Flutterwave plan purchases provide 30 days of access and require manual renewal; they do not automatically renew. Paddle subscriptions renew monthly. Manage or cancel a Paddle subscription through the provider’s customer portal or contact Support for help.

How do I change or recover my password?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page to request a reset email. To change a password while signed in, open Profile & settings and choose Change password. Never share your password or verification code with anyone.

How can I keep my account and files secure?

Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices and keep original copies of important files. Only upload information you are authorised to share. Never send passwords, one-time codes, API keys or full payment-card details to Support.

How do I report a security or privacy concern?

Email tampslat@tampconsulting.space with the affected page or tool, approximate time and a non-sensitive description. Do not attach passwords, API keys, payment details or confidential document contents.

How do I request billing help or account deletion?

Contact Support from the email address associated with your account. Include the approximate date, plan name and transaction reference if relevant. For data correction or deletion, describe the account request; Support will verify ownership before processing it.

What does each plan include?

Every plan can access every studio and supported browser-based tool. Free includes 3 account saves and 10 server-side CSV quality checks per month; Starter includes 100 of each, Pro 500 of each and Business 2,000 of each. AI credits are separate. Local file processing, including OCR, is not metered.

How is a saved work item counted?

Each successful Save to account action creates one account item and uses one monthly save. Deleting an item does not restore that allowance. Drafts and files kept only in your browser do not count as account saves.

Need further support?

Contact us directly. Include the tool name and a non-sensitive error message when reporting an issue. Never send passwords, full payment-card details, API keys or confidential documents.
